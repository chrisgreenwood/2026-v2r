## Event Overview

- **Event:** Casey Folks Vegas to Reno — "The Other Way" (30th anniversary)
- **Dates:** Aug 11–15, 2026
- **Format twist:** For the 30th anniversary, the race runs **reversed** — normally
  Vegas→Reno, this year it's **Dayton, NV (start) → Crystal, NV (finish)**.
- **Distance:** 505 miles, longest point-to-point off-road race in the US
- **Pits:** 11 numbered pits along the course
- **Organizer:** Best in the Desert (BitD) / American Off-Road Racing Championship (AORC)
- **Official pages:** bitd.com/v2r/, /schedule/, /entrylist/, /race-format/, /pit-information-guide/
  — verified live against these pages Aug 12, 2026; public schedule and pit closing
  times match this doc exactly.
